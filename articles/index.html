Power Engineering Guides

Written for Canadian operators building a career around a ticket: passing the papers, then using them. Everything here is free and open, no sign-up.

  • Exams: what is on each SOPEEC paper, section by section, and where candidates lose marks.
  • Study methods: how to prepare around a rotation, when your study time comes in fifteen-minute pieces.
  • Jobs and hiring: who is actually hiring by province, drawn from real postings, plus resumes and interviews.
  • Pay and career: what each certificate is worth by province and by sector, and what the next one unlocks.

Most of this is built from primary sources: the current SOPEEC syllabus, published regulator fee schedules, federal wage data, and the live postings on our own job board. Where a number is an estimate, we say so.
