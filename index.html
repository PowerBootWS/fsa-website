Common Questions

Questions From the Field

Quick answers to what working operators ask before enrolling.

Haven't opened a textbook in years?

That's exactly who it's built for. Step-by-step coaching and AI tutoring assume no recent classroom experience; just your field knowledge as a starting point.

Do I need to complete every paper for my certificate?

Yes. All the SOPEEC papers for your certificate are required: six for 2nd Class, four for 3rd Class, and 4A plus 4B for 4th Class. The 2nd and 3rd Class subscriptions include every paper for the ticket, so there is no per-paper charge on either. 4th Class works differently: 4A and 4B are sold separately at $99 each for the year, so you can buy only the paper you're writing.

What's included?

On 2nd and 3rd Class, every paper for your certificate: step-by-step calculation coaching, guided video explanations, interactive AI tutoring, adaptive practice exams, and progress tracking. 4th Class is exam prep rather than a full course: adaptive practice, chapter quizzes and a worked explanation on every question you miss. Study on any device.

Can I cancel and come back later?

Yes. There are no contracts or minimum terms. Cancel anytime and your study progress is always saved; resubscribe when your schedule allows.

How long does it take to get through a paper?

Most working operators spend 4–8 weeks per paper depending on shift schedule and how much prior review they need. The full course typically runs 6–12 months.

Is this aligned to ABSA and SOPEEC?

Yes. Content is structured around the published exam objectives as set out by SOPEEC for interprovincial certification, across 4th, 3rd and 2nd Class. Practice exams reflect how questions are actually marked.
