Why this beats re-reading your notes

A Chapter Test Doesn't Ask If You Read the Unit. It Asks If You Can Use It.

Most students prep for a chapter test by rereading the unit or skimming old notes. That tells you what you were told, not what you actually know. The only way to find the gap is to get tested on it, and the only way to close it is to be shown exactly why the answer you picked was wrong.

That's what FSA does. Every practice question is pulled from your paper's material. Every wrong answer comes with a full explanation, not just the correct choice. And the more you practice, the more the system weights toward the topics you're actually missing, so your remaining study time goes to your weak spots instead of the stuff you already have down.
